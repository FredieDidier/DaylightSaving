Author: Leonardo Monasterio (Instituto de Pesquisa Econ\^omica Aplicada (Ipea) and Instituto Brasileiro de Ensino, Desenvolvimento e Pesquisa (IDP))

Article: Darker Evenings: Road Deaths after Brazil Abolished Daylight Saving Time

I am employed by Ipea, a public foundation of the Brazilian federal government linked to the Ministry of Planning and Budget. The federal government abolished daylight saving time in 2019, and the article uses a cost of road deaths published by Ipea.

I received no financial support for the research described in this article. In the past three years I have received no significant financial or in-kind support from any individual, group, or organization with a financial, ideological, or political stake related to the article, other than my salary from Ipea. I hold no paid or unpaid position as officer, director, or board member of an organization whose policy positions, goals, or financial interests relate to the article. The same is true of my close relatives and partner.

No other party had the right to review the article before its circulation.

The research uses only publicly available data and involved no collection of data on human subjects, so Institutional Review Board approval was not sought.

I have nothing else to disclose.
